Biggest feedback
A graph is only as good as the data behind its connections.
With 50 sample rows, foods could not be connected. With the full dataset, “similar food” appeared on its own. Better swaps need still more data: health guidelines, recipes and allergens.
Next: add those layers, then a plain-language agent with memory.
